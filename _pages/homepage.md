<h2 id="about-me" style="margin-top: 0;"><i class="fas fa-address-card fa-fw"></i> About Me</h2>

I am a fourth-year Ph.D. student at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Beihang University. 

My research primarily focuses on **3D computer vision**, **intelligent measurement systems**, and **point cloud processing**. I am dedicated to developing high-precision, automated 3D scanning and measurement pipelines. Specifically, my work spans three core areas:

* <i class="fas fa-cubes fa-fw"></i> **Measurement System Construction.** Developing multi-view registration measurement systems based on global collaborative localization, network optimization, and phase matching.
* <i class="fas fa-laptop-code fa-fw"></i> **Simulation & Uncertainty Modeling.** Building structured light simulation platforms for physical modeling, data-driven uncertainty quantification, and evaluating viewpoint quality based on error fields.
* <i class="fas fa-brain fa-fw"></i> **Intelligent Viewpoint Planning.** Proposing intelligent viewpoint optimization and planning strategies under strict accuracy constraints for complex curvature surfaces, covering aspects from local single field-of-view measurement optimization to global Next-Best-Scan planning based on Deep Reinforcement Learning.
